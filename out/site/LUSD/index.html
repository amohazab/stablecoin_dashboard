The admin-power table, row by row

No admin power can alter backing — immutable. The protocol lacks any holder for minting, debt ceilings, contract upgrades, pausing, freezing assets, blacklisting addresses, oracle settings, risk parameters, or seizing assets. Each of these powers is held by no one and marked as immutable.

Any change to these functions would require a modification that does not exist in the current code. The delay for every potential action is listed as no delay because the authority to act is absent. No veto address exists to block a change, and no owner contract holds the keys to alter the system.

The structure remains fixed at the report's block. Because no entity controls minting or backing adjustments, the collateral ratio cannot be changed by an administrator. The absence of these powers means the token's value relies entirely on market forces rather than administrative intervention.
