The counterfactual where the price feed falls back from Chainlink to the Tellor oracle affects the measure that relies on instant observation of the PriceFeed at the report's block. This change alters how the price basis is calculated, shifting it from a bundle's own total collateral value divided by the total collateral amount to a re-simulated fresh price-feed read. The metric changes because only a fresh price-feed read rewrites the last good price, whereas the fallback introduces a lagged moving-average price line that does not exist in Liquity v1.
