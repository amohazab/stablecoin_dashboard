A depeg of the paired stablecoin fits this token's mechanism because it is structurally insulated by construction, meaning its internal collateral ratio does not change during a shock. Instead, any impact flows through exit venues where liquidity providers may withdraw their assets. The recomputed exit-liquidity curves show that at a price impact of 5.0%, the available depth is $23.7M when the paired stablecoin trades at 0.93.

The system also holds $20.6M in burn capacity and maintains $80.7M of effective PegKeeper headroom that can actually be used to manage stress. These resources remain available even as the paired asset moves toward its depeg target of 0xdac17f…1ec7.

Liquidity providers staying put contribute to the depth curve, but their flight levels determine how quickly usable liquidity shrinks under pressure.

No one holds the power to upgrade the contract, freeze assets, blacklist addresses, or seize funds. The contract is immutable by design, meaning its core rules cannot be altered once deployed.
