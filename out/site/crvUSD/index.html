The admin-power table, row by row

The DAO holds the power to mint new tokens, set debt ceilings, adjust oracle settings, and modify risk parameters. Each of these changes takes between one and seven days to apply, as indicated by a delay of 1 to 7 day delay for minting and debt ceilings, and a 1 to 7 day delay for oracle and risk parameter adjustments. The DAO also holds the authority to pause operations instantly with no delay, though this power is held by a specific address that acts on behalf of the DAO.

A separate entity, identified as 0x467947…1e0c, can veto changes to debt ceilings and oracle settings but has no veto over minting. This veto holder cannot pause operations or freeze assets, as no one holds the power to freeze assets or seize tokens. The contract itself is immutable, meaning no one holds the power to upgrade it, and blacklisting addresses is not a function available within this system.

The scope of these administrative powers covers specific contracts, including 0xc9332f…38bc and 0x36a04c…855f. While the DAO controls most critical functions like minting and parameter setting, the presence of a dedicated veto address for debt and oracle changes introduces a check that prevents unilateral action by the DAO on those specific levers.
